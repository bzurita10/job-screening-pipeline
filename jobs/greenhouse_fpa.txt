FP&A Analyst — Greenhouse
Anywhere in the United States (remote-first)

Greenhouse is looking for an FP&A Analyst to join our team. The Revenue FP&A team plays a critical role in guiding strategic decision-making and operational execution across our Go-To-Market organization. As an FP&A Analyst, you will serve as the primary owner of day-to-day forecasting, reporting, and variance analysis for Sales and RevOps headcount and operating expenses. Your work will directly shape our understanding of CGS performance and help connect headcount and expense assumptions directly to topline results. You will focus on executional excellence in financial modeling, headcount tracking, and routine reporting, building driver-based models and delivering actionable insights.

What you'll do:
- Own detailed forecasting, budgeting, and variance analysis for Sales and RevOps headcount and operating expenses
- Build and maintain driver-based financial models and monthly forecast updates
- Prepare and refresh monthly and quarterly headcount and OpEx reporting for GTM leadership with clear explanations of variance drivers, risks, and opportunities
- Support revenue and ARR forecasting by linking headcount and expense assumptions to topline capacity and performance
- Partner closely with Sales, RevOps, Accounting, Procurement, and broader FP&A teams

You should have:
- 2-4 years of experience in FP&A, investment banking, consulting, or a related analytical role
- Strong financial modeling and Excel/Sheets skills with experience in forecasting, budgeting, and variance analysis
- Comfort working with headcount and OpEx data to tie HR snapshots, hiring plans, and expense actuals into a coherent forecast
- Experience experimenting with AI tools and interest in integrating them thoughtfully into day-to-day work
- Strong written and verbal communication skills with the ability to convey insights to non-finance partners
- Bachelor's degree in Finance, Accounting, Economics, Business, or related quantitative field, a plus
- Experience in SaaS or recurring revenue environments, a plus
- Experience partnering directly with Sales or RevOps teams, a plus
- Familiarity with financial planning and reporting tools, a plus

The national pay range for this role is $79,300 - $97,200. Remote-first company. Anticipated closing date: August 31, 2026.
